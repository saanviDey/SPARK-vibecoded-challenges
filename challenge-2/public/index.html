    How we play

    Match the active color or symbol. Wild cards choose a color. Skip and Reverse affect turns; with two players Reverse acts as Skip. +2 and +4 draw cards and skip the next player. No stacking. +4 requires no card of the active color. Draw one, then play only that card or pass. Tick Call UNO before playing down to one card, or draw two as a penalty. Turns last 60 seconds. First empty hand wins. Host can start a rematch. Refresh to reconnect to your seat.
